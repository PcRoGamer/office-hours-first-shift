ASTER // FIELD NOTES

Why make work into play?

We wanted an ordinary office job to be enjoyable to touch, not just a list of tasks. What if a silly action you master now could change the next challenge?

People encounter repetitive instructions and rewards every day. Games let us experiment with those rules: the choice matters more than the points alone.

01 / QUICK SURPRISEDumb Ways to Die (2013) ↗

Short, funny actions communicate immediately. Here, those actions also carry consequences forward; failure is comic and recoverable.

02 / BUILDS THAT CHANGE PLAYBrotato (2023) ↗

Between-wave choices make a run your own. We apply that rhythm to varied office toys instead of another combat arena.

03 / THE JOB AS A TOYJob Simulator (2016) ↗

Handling ordinary objects is fun before the score. Our mouse-driven 2D desk makes that tactile idea fit a quick browser game, without copying its VR hands.

Story influence: Papers, Please (2013) uses bureaucracy to tell a story; its document-checking is not a mechanic here. The office, people and figures in this game are fictional.
